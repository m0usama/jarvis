Learning Moment: Embeddings and Vector Databases

  • An embedding is a list of floating-point numbers mapping the semantic meaning of text into a high-dimensional mathematical space.
  • Concepts with similar meanings are positioned physically close together, matching context even when exact keywords differ.
  • Vector databases (like Chroma or SQLite-based vector extensions) store these coordinates to run fast similarity and angular distance searches across millions of documents.
  • Example: In an automated print catalogue pipeline, embedding raw email inquiries lets the system match customer wording like "thick brochure print run" to past job records like "twenty-four page catalogue on heavy silk stock" based on vector proximity rather than strict keywords.
