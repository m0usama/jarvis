Learning Moment: Logging, tracing, and observability for LLM applications

  • Traditional software uses status codes and error logs, but LLM applications require continuous observability due to non-deterministic outputs, varying latencies, and silent failures.
  • Observability relies on three pillars: Logs (raw prompts and outputs), Metrics (token counts, latency, API costs), and Traces (execution step sequences and step timings).
  • Tracing allows engineers to isolate delayed steps, debug truncated outputs, alert on latency spikes, audit prompt histories, and optimize token costs.
  • Example: In a news briefing pipeline taking 15 seconds, trace spans reveal whether the delay comes from fetching RSS feeds, waiting on the LLM, or writing output files, without crashing the application.
