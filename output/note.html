Learning Moment: Embeddings and Vector Databases

  • An embedding translates text into a vector (list of numbers) where similar meanings sit close together mathematically.
  • Replaces literal keyword queries by measuring closeness between a question's vector and stored text vectors.
  • Vector databases store millions of vectors and execute rapid similarity searches for nearest neighbours.
  • Serves as the core retrieval step to supply relevant business context to an LLM before generation.
  • Example: A search for "protective outdoor signs" successfully matches "durable weather-resistant vinyl banner" based on conceptual vector direction rather than exact keywords.
