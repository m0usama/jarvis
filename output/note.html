Learning Moment: Chunking Strategies and Why Retrieval Fails

  • RAG systems rarely fail because of vector math; they fail due to improper document chunking prior to embedding.
  • Overly large chunks dilute semantic embeddings, while overly small chunks strip context and split critical thoughts.
  • Production systems utilize recursive character or document-aware chunking to respect natural boundaries like paragraphs and tables.
  • Example: A fixed 200-character split cuts a print manual sentence between general card stock limits and a warning on textured stock, causing retrieval to fetch only the first clause and generate damaging instructions.
